I am establishing a research group in the **School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore**, beginning in **January 2027**.

We will study the physics of **complex fluids, from turbulence to biological fluids**. The group will combine experiments, microscopy and flow visualization, quantitative analysis, and physical modeling.

## Research directions

- **Biomolecular condensates and experimental biophysics:** phase separation, solute regulation, and reaction-driven condensates.
- **Fluid mechanics and turbulence:** nonlinear transport, turbulent spreading and decay, and multiphase or reactive flows.
- **Soft and living matter:** emergent dynamics and fluid–material interactions in nonequilibrium systems.

Candidates interested in bridging experimental physics, mechanical engineering, biophysics, and quantitative methods are especially encouraged to get in touch.

## PhD students

I welcome inquiries from prospective PhD students interested in joining the group, including candidates considering the **2027 intake**. Admission and funding are subject to NTU's official graduate admissions process and the availability of positions.

Please send a brief introduction, your CV, and a summary of your research interests. If available, include a transcript and examples of prior research.

## Postdoctoral researchers

I am interested in hearing from prospective postdoctoral researchers with backgrounds in **experimental biophysics / soft matter** or **fluid mechanics / turbulence**. The timing and availability of positions will be confirmed through formal NTU recruitment announcements.

Please include your CV, a short description of your research contributions, and a statement of the problems you would like to explore.

## How to get in touch

For initial inquiries, email **[tmatsuzawa@cornell.edu](mailto:tmatsuzawa@cornell.edu)** with a subject such as **Prospective PhD student – NTU** or **Prospective postdoc – NTU**.

Please note that an inquiry does not constitute a formal application or an offer of admission or employment. Official application procedures will be provided as appropriate.
